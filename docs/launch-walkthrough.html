Launch walkthrough: go-live 1 October 2026

Everything that has to happen, in the order it has to happen, to take the portal live and run its first month. Each step names who does it, where, what to enter and what you should see. Tick a step when it matches; your ticks stay in this browser only.

Before you begin

  • Site: dommus-negotii-staging.vercel.app. Staff sign in on the Staff tab with an email, password and authenticator code; members use the Member tab with a phone number.
  • Staff logins are the demo.<role>@dommus.invalid addresses in docs/demo-files/logins-public.json. Their passwords are only in .env.demo.local on your computer.
  • The files are in docs/launch-files: L1 to L5 are the data files, and documents/ holds one document per upload (D1 to D8) and one ID scan per person. Use each document once: the system raises a duplicate-evidence exception when the same file backs two different submissions.
  • Staging sends no real SMS or email. Admin reads every message under Staging outbox. From 11 November the outbox also shows the alerts each step produces: the member's SMS (application received, loan approved, payment applied, refund paid) and the email telling the next staff role that something waits for them.
  • Use one normal window for staff and a private window for members, so two people can be signed in at once.
  • These steps were rehearsed end to end on the development database on 10 November 2026 and every figure matched.
  • Do it once, in order. Staging keeps what you post (the ledger can't be undone), and the older demo data stays visible beside the launch data.
  • Today on staging is 9 November 2026, so the payroll, bank and month end for October all happen “after the fact”, the way a real launch catches up.
