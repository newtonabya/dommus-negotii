Two-person rules and roles that can't be combined

One prepares, another verifies
  • Loan: Loan Officer verifies and recommends → Management approves
  • Disbursement voucher: Cashier → Finance Manager
  • Schedule correction: Treasurer → Finance Manager
  • Source Advice (Finance Manager), when given, is checked against the schedule (Treasurer)
  • Guarantee: the guarantor accepts by their own SMS link before the loan is verified, approved or paid out
  • Bank charge, interest or transfer: Cashier → Finance Manager
  • Bank opening balance: Admin → Finance Manager
  • Financial setting: Admin proposes → Management approves
  • Lodgement: Finance Manager verifies at the bank → Cashier records
Never the same person
  • Cashier + Finance Manager
  • Treasurer + Finance Manager
  • Management + Cashier
  • Management + Loan Officer
  • Secretary + Loan Officer
  • Admin or GMD with any other role
  • Staff never act on their own loan or membership

Each follows a real situation across the actors involved, with what should happen and the mistakes the system must refuse.

1

From invitation to member

Secretary → Applicant → Admin (outbox) → Loan Officer → Member

  1. Secretary: Invitations → invite a new person (any 080… number) with source DEMO-PAYROLL.
  2. Admin: Staging outbox → copy the invitation link; open it in a private window.
  3. Applicant: fill the form (a wrong next-of-kin phone or a common password is refused before any code is sent), upload 7-sample-id-card.jpg, set a password, then enter the code from the outbox.
  4. Loan Officer: Members → record the payroll staff number (e.g. S05001) → Activate.
  5. Member: sign in on the Member tab.

ExpectThe member is ACTIVE with the next MOAUM number (MOAUM/00…); the “registration received” and welcome SMS both give it, and Home shows it with the PIN.

Try to break it
  • Loan Officer: try Activate before recording the staff number (refused).
  • Applicant: open the same link again after registering (refused).
  • Secretary: invite the same phone twice (refused).

The two-active-loan limit

  1. Member with one active loan (e.g. Amina): apply twice more; both are submitted (applications in review don't count).
  2. Guarantor: accept both by the SMS links in the outbox. Loan Officer: verify and recommend both. The panel reads “Active loans: 1 of 2 allowed”.
  3. Management: approve the first; approve the second.
  4. Management: cancel the approved loan and reject the other, so nothing is paid out.

ExpectThe second approval is refused: “The member already has 1 active loan and 1 approved loan waiting to be paid; the limit is 2 active loans.” The setting is Active loans per member (default 2); guarantors are limited the same way.

Try to break it

  • Cashier: prepare a payout that would make a third active loan (refused).

Checking the SMS account (Termii)

You, on your own computer (not a portal role)

  1. Create .env.termii.local in the dommus-negotii folder (git-ignored; type the key in yourself, never into a chat or a document): TERMII_API_KEY, TERMII_SENDER_ID (e.g. MOAUM), TERMII_BASE_URL (as your Termii dashboard shows it), TERMII_ROUTE=dnd.
  2. Run: npx --yes tsx@4.23.15 scripts/termii-check.mts (free; sends nothing).
  3. Once the sender ID is approved: npx --yes tsx@4.23.15 scripts/termii-check.mts --send 0803XXXXXXX, first to your own phone, then to a phone on DND.

Expect“✓ The key works. Wallet balance: NGN …”, “✓ Sender ID … is approved”, and after --send “✓ Termii accepted the SMS”, with the message arriving from your sender ID, on the DND phone too. The key is never printed. Staging itself still sends nothing: every message stays in the Staging outbox.

Try to break it
  • Put a wrong key in the file (“✗ The key was not accepted”, and nothing else runs).
  • Use a sender ID Termii hasn't approved yet (it says so: wait before sending).
  • --send with a number that isn't Nigerian (refused before anything is sent).

The launch walkthrough

This guide tests each role on the DEMO-PAYROLL data. The launch walkthrough takes the portal live in order on its own launch data (files in docs/launch-files), and covers what this guide doesn't:

  • Setting the go-live date and the bank's go-live opening balance, then reconciling and closing October to the kobo (bank ₦2,008,395.00, ledger ₦1,995,895.00, difference ₦0.00).
  • The legacy loan list creating its borrowers as members, each finishing their own set-up (address, next of kin) from the SMS link.
  • A November payroll where one member overpays, one underpays and one clears a loan, with every figure rehearsed on the development database.
  • December's deduction schedule after that payroll, and the loan limit, all with exact figures to check.
  • A real payroll line shared across two loans on staging (SCH/REF/0002, Newton Abya), and a member's history with one loan completed (Phase 10).
  • The account-number check on the loan application, the payout voucher and both refund forms, step by step.

Its steps can be ticked as you go; ticks stay in your browser.

Not built yet

  • Real SMS and email delivery: staging writes every message to the Staging outbox instead. The Termii account can be checked from your computer first (scenario 22); production then gets its four values, and Resend's for email.
  • A name check of a bank account with the bank (needs a payment provider); the account number is only checked against the bank's check digit.
Demo data on staging is permanent (the ledger can't be edited) and will not exist in production, which starts from a clean database.
